About

Designing across
dimensions

Haotian Shen is a designer working at the intersection of visual storytelling, extended reality, brand identity, and immersive experience. With a deep curiosity for how design shapes perception, Haotian brings conceptual rigour and craft to every project.

From spatial narratives in VR to editorial systems for film, from restaurant identities to experimental motion work — the common thread is always story: how meaning is made through image, space, and form.

Currently available for freelance collaborations and full-time opportunities in design, XR, and creative direction.
