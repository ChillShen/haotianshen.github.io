Emotion World

This project is structured around three distinct virtual environments, each defined by its own set of rules and emotional conditions. The first environment introduces pressure through time constraints, task-based interaction, and visible consequences, creating a heightened sense of urgency and anxiety. The second removes these constraints entirely, offering an open and responsive space where interaction is optional and the experience shifts toward calmness and reflection. The third environment focuses on movement and play, translating user input into continuous motion and dynamic feedback, encouraging a sense of freedom and flow.

Together, these three worlds form a spectrum of experience — from control and tension, to release, and finally to immersion through movement — demonstrating how different systems of rules can shape perception, behavior, and emotional response within virtual space.

User Experience

The video captures the user’s journey across three distinct environments, each shaped by a different set of rules and emotional conditions. As the experience unfolds, shifts in space, sound, and interaction guide the user from pressure and constraint, to calm and openness, and finally to movement and flow.

Rather than presenting a fixed narrative, the experience is driven by real-time interaction. Subtle changes in perception and feedback create moments of tension, release, and immersion, revealing how different systems can influence behavior and emotional response.
